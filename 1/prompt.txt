TASK: SON TRA PENINSULA — EXTERIOR VISUAL TRANSFORMATION
Project: https://danangbridges.netlify.app/
Technology: Existing Three.js project.
Primary objective: Transform the external appearance of the existing Son Tra Peninsula from artificial block-like geometry into a realistic, naturally shaped tropical mountainous landscape.
1. VISION
The current Son Tra Peninsula looks like a collection of artificial geometric blocks.
I want to replace this appearance with a realistic mountain landscape inspired by the actual Son Tra Peninsula in Da Nang, Vietnam.
The most important requirement:
When users stand on Dragon Bridge, Han River Bridge, Tran Thi Ly Bridge or Thuan Phuoc Bridge and look toward Son Tra, they should immediately recognize a beautiful green mountainous peninsula instead of artificial blocks.
This is an exterior visual enhancement only.
Do NOT turn this task into a new open-world environment project.
Do NOT build detailed environments inside the peninsula.
Do NOT introduce unnecessary gameplay features.

3. MOUNTAIN SILHOUETTE — HIGHEST PRIORITY
Replace the current artificial block-like mountain appearance.
Create a naturally connected mountain formation with:

* Multiple irregular mountain peaks.
* Continuous mountain ridges.
* Natural slopes.
* Uneven terrain elevations.
* Organic valleys between mountains.
* Gradual transitions between mountain sections.
* Realistic mountain silhouettes.

The mountain silhouette must resemble the real Son Tra Peninsula.
Avoid:

* Cubes.
* Rectangular blocks.
* Stacked geometric platforms.
* Repeated pyramid-shaped mountains.
* Artificially smooth domes.
* Clearly visible mesh boundaries.
* Abrupt elevation changes.

Use an appropriate terrain mesh, displacement approach or other efficient Three.js technique based on the existing architecture.
The result must look like one continuous natural landscape, not several disconnected geometric objects.
4. NATURAL FOREST APPEARANCE
The peninsula should appear predominantly covered by dense tropical rainforest.
I do not need individual trees modeled in extensive detail.
Instead, prioritize convincing forest appearance from a distance.
Use suitable combinations of:

* Dense canopy clusters.
* Natural vegetation textures.
* Procedural vegetation distributions where useful.
* Subtle variations in green.
* Different canopy heights.
* Natural forest silhouettes.
* Layered vegetation shading.

Avoid repetitive patterns.
Avoid a uniform flat-green material.
The mountains should have the visual appearance of a real tropical forest covering uneven mountainous terrain.
Focus on the overall visual impression rather than individual vegetation details.
5. TERRAIN MATERIALS AND NATURAL DETAILS
Improve the mountain surface with:

* Natural forest-green materials.
* Subtle exposed rock formations.
* Darker vegetation in shadowed areas.
* Lighter vegetation in sunlit areas.
* Natural color variation.
* Subtle terrain texture.
* Smooth material blending.

Use realistic lighting and physically appropriate material properties where supported by the existing rendering pipeline.
Do not introduce excessive texture complexity.
The terrain should remain visually convincing at the actual viewing distances of the existing scene.
6. REALISTIC ATMOSPHERIC APPEARANCE
Make the peninsula blend naturally into the existing Da Nang environment.
Pay attention to:

* Sunlight direction.
* Mountain shadows.
* Atmospheric depth.
* Distance-based color variation.
* Natural green tones.
* Subtle haze where appropriate.

The mountain should not look like a separately imported object floating in the scene.
It must visually integrate with the existing ocean, city and sky.
Reuse existing environmental lighting.
Do not create a separate weather or lighting system.
