# TASK: UPGRADE PARAGLIDING HUMAN CHARACTER TO PUBG-INSPIRED REALISTIC 3D QUALITY

## 1. PROJECT CONTEXT

Existing project:
https://danangbridges.netlify.app/

This is an existing Three.js-based 3D interactive experience showcasing Da Nang bridges and the Han River.

The project already has a functional paragliding mode. However, the current human character attached to the paraglider looks stiff, artificial, mannequin-like, and lacks realistic human anatomy and movement.

Your mission is to upgrade the existing paragliding character into a highly realistic, PUBG-inspired human character.

**IMPORTANT: This is an enhancement of an existing feature, NOT a new project.**

Use the Superpowers workflow to:
1. Inspect the existing codebase and architecture.
2. Identify the current paragliding implementation.
3. Analyze the existing character model, rigging, animation, paraglider, camera and physics.
4. Create a detailed implementation plan.
5. Implement the upgrade.
6. Test and optimize the result.

Do not stop after producing a specification. Continue through implementation and validation wherever possible without unnecessary human approval requests.

---

## 2. VISUAL DIRECTION — PUBG-INSPIRED REALISM

Use PUBG's realistic military-survival character aesthetic as the primary visual reference.

The result should resemble a high-quality human character from a modern realistic 3D survival game.

Do NOT create:
- Cartoon characters.
- Low-poly characters.
- Roblox-style characters.
- Mannequin-like bodies.
- Oversized heads or unrealistic proportions.
- Simplified cylindrical arms and legs.

### Human anatomy

The character must have:
- Realistic adult human body proportions.
- Anatomically believable shoulders, chest, waist and hips.
- Natural elbow and knee joints.
- Realistic hands with individual fingers.
- Properly modeled feet and footwear.
- Natural neck and head proportions.
- Realistic clothing folds and fabric thickness.

The character must look like a real human wearing professional paragliding equipment.

### Character appearance

Preferred appearance:
- Adult male character.
- Realistic tactical outdoor clothing.
- Dark olive, charcoal, black and muted military colors.
- Detailed tactical gloves.
- Realistic boots.
- Outdoor jacket and functional trousers.
- Helmet with realistic surface materials.
- Professional paragliding harness.
- Detailed straps, buckles and attachment points.
- Realistic backpack and equipment.

Avoid excessive military weapons or aggressive combat accessories. This is a scenic paragliding experience, not a combat game.

The character should look like a PUBG-quality outdoor survival character adapted for recreational paragliding.

Do not copy PUBG's proprietary characters, logos, textures or assets. Create an original character inspired by its visual quality.

## 3. CHARACTER RIGGING AND ANIMATION

This is the most important part of the upgrade.

A realistic static model is insufficient.

The character must behave like a real person suspended underneath a paraglider.

### Required animation behavior

**A. Default flying pose**
- Natural seated/suspended posture.
- Slightly bent knees.
- Relaxed but believable arms.
- Hands naturally holding the paraglider brake toggles.
- Feet positioned naturally.
- Subtle body movement caused by wind and flight.

**B. Wind reaction**
- Torso reacts subtly to wind.
- Legs move naturally with air resistance.
- Clothing and backpack exhibit secondary movement.
- Harness straps remain attached to the character.
- Small movements should be continuous and organic.

**C. Turning**
When the paraglider turns left or right:
- The character's torso leans naturally.
- Shoulders rotate with the body.
- Legs follow the turning momentum.
- Arms react naturally.
- The entire body should not rotate like a rigid object.

**D. Acceleration and deceleration**
- Body leans slightly according to acceleration.
- Legs react to changes in speed.
- Harness and clothing respond to movement.
- Avoid exaggerated animation.

**E. Camera interaction**
The character must remain visually convincing from different camera angles.

Avoid visible animation glitches, clipping, disconnected limbs or unnatural joint rotations.

---

## 4. PARAGLIDER AND EQUIPMENT INTEGRATION

The character must be physically integrated with the existing paraglider.

Requirements:
- Correct attachment between harness and paraglider suspension lines.
- Suspension lines must connect to believable attachment points.
- Harness must fit naturally around the character's body.
- Backpack must remain attached to the character.
- Brake handles must align with the character's hands.
- Equipment must follow the character's movement.

The entire system should look like one coherent physical setup.

Do not simply position a human model beneath the canopy without realistic attachment points.

---

## 5. TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION

The existing project uses Three.js.

Preserve the existing technology stack and architecture.

Preferred implementation:

- Three.js.
- GLTF/GLB animated character models.
- SkinnedMesh and Skeleton.
- AnimationMixer and AnimationAction.
- Physically Based Rendering (PBR).
- Realistic material maps.
- Proper shadow and lighting integration.

If a suitable rigged character model is needed, evaluate legitimate sources of production-quality assets.

Possible sources:
- Sketchfab (check commercial and redistribution licenses).
- Mixamo (for compatible rigging and animations).
- Ready Player Me (only if visual quality meets the target).
- Original custom GLB assets.

Do not assume external assets are already available.

If external assets cannot be downloaded or integrated automatically, create a clear asset acquisition specification and implement the best available solution without pretending that placeholder geometry is production-ready.

### Animation architecture

Create a modular character animation controller.

Suggested responsibilities:

- CharacterLoader: Load and initialize the GLB character.
- CharacterAnimator: Manage animation states.
- CharacterRigController: Control bones and body posture.
- FlightPoseController: Handle the default suspended flying pose.
- FlightReactionController: Handle wind, turning and acceleration reactions.
- EquipmentController: Synchronize harness, straps and equipment.

Adapt these names to the existing architecture rather than unnecessarily creating duplicate systems.

Use delta time for frame-rate-independent animation.

Avoid excessive per-frame object allocation.

## 6. REALISM AND VISUAL QUALITY

Target visual quality: modern realistic 3D survival game.

Pay attention to:

- PBR material quality.
- Realistic roughness and metallic properties.
- Natural skin and clothing appearance.
- Fabric folds.
- Equipment details.
- Contact shadows.
- Ambient occlusion where appropriate.
- Correct lighting response.
- Natural body silhouette.

The character must look convincing when viewed:
- From behind.
- From the side.
- From the front.
- From a distant third-person camera.
- From a closer inspection camera.

Do not sacrifice the performance of the existing Da Nang 3D environment to achieve character quality.

---

## 7. PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS

The project is a browser-based Three.js experience.

Maintain smooth performance on modern desktop browsers.

Requirements:
- Optimize GLB model size.
- Use compressed textures where appropriate.
- Avoid unnecessary skeleton complexity.
- Reuse materials.
- Dispose of resources correctly.
- Avoid memory leaks when switching camera modes.
- Ensure character animation does not negatively affect the existing scene.
- Provide sensible quality fallback options.

Target smooth rendering at 60 FPS on reasonably capable desktop hardware, without treating this as a guarantee across all devices.
