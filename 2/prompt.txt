### Core concept

**"DA NANG — EXPERIENCE THE CITY FROM A DIFFERENT PERSPECTIVE"**

**"ĐÀ NẴNG — KHÁM PHÁ THÀNH PHỐ THEO CÁCH CỦA BẠN"**

3. Discovery — The small details, environmental effects, dynamic bridges, and realistic atmosphere that make the experience special.

### Visual identity

Use a combination of:

- Cinematic aerial camera movements.
- Smooth first-person perspectives.
- Dynamic tracking shots.
- Wide-angle establishing shots.
- Beautiful close-up details.
- Smooth time transitions.
- Dramatic but tasteful lighting.
- Professional cinematic editing.

The video should feel like a premium travel experience combined with a modern interactive 3D product showcase.

Avoid the appearance of a simple screen recording or a basic software tutorial.

### Technical output

- Duration: Target 90 seconds, acceptable range 80–110 seconds.
- Aspect ratio: 16:9.
- Resolution: 1920 × 1080 minimum.
- Frame rate: 30 FPS.
- Format: MP4, H.264.
- Audio: AAC.
- Platform: YouTube.
- Use cinematic color grading while preserving the original appearance and identity of the website.
- Use clean, elegant typography.
- Avoid excessive text and unnecessary interface elements.

## 6. CINEMATIC CAMERA & RECORDING GUIDELINES

This section is critical.

The footage must look professionally directed rather than randomly captured.

### Camera movement

Use different camera techniques:

- Slow aerial flyovers.
- Smooth forward tracking shots.
- First-person movement.
- Side tracking shots.
- Wide establishing shots.
- Low-angle architectural shots.
- Close-up detail shots.
- Slow cinematic reveals.
- Smooth transitions between viewpoints.

### Recording rules

- Use a sufficiently high-resolution browser viewport.
- Prefer recording at 60 FPS when the available environment supports stable capture, then export at 30 FPS.
- Avoid visible mouse movement unless it is needed to demonstrate a specific interaction.
- Do not leave menus, loading indicators, error messages, or debugging panels on screen.
- Wait until each scene is fully loaded and visually stable before recording.
- Keep camera movement smooth.
- Avoid excessive camera rotation or sudden changes in direction.
- Make sure important objects remain in frame.
- Capture multiple takes when necessary and select the best footage.

### Cinematic composition

Use:

- Balanced composition.
- Clear visual focal points.
- Attractive lighting.
- Appropriate depth and perspective.
- Natural contrast.
- Smooth transitions.

Avoid:

- Random camera movement.
- Overly fast cuts.
- Excessive motion blur.
- Excessive zooming.
- Overuse of transition effects.
- Distorted or stretched visuals.
- Shots that do not clearly communicate a feature.
